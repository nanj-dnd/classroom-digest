Everything due, on one page.

Sign in with the Google account your Classroom is on. You get every course's open work sorted by deadline, deadlines professors only wrote in announcements, what was posted recently, and your latest grades.

Sign in with Google

What it shows

  • Overdue work, then what's due in the next day, three days and week
  • Dates found in announcement text that were never set as assignments
  • Announcements and materials from the last two weeks
  • Recently graded work
  • Hide tasks you don't care about, and courses from past semesters
  • An email digest on the days and at the time you pick, with only what you want in it

Use it from Claude

Add Classroom Digest as a connector and ask your own Claude things like "what's due this week?" or "what did my Macro professor announce?". Claude reads your Classroom directly. It runs on your Claude plan, with no API key.

  • claude.ai or the Claude app: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, then paste https://college.anshul-anand.com/mcp
  • Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http classroom https://college.anshul-anand.com/mcp, then run /mcp to sign in

Either way you sign in with Google once. The connector is read-only, like the site.

What it stores

Only your settings: hidden tasks and courses, and your email choices, encrypted. Your Google sign-in is kept on the server only while email reminders are on, so they can be sent; otherwise it stays in an encrypted cookie in your browser. Your coursework is read from Classroom when you open the page or when an email goes out, and never saved. Access is read-only. Delete everything any time in Settings.

Want synced files, calendar deadlines or study help? The open-source version runs on your own computer and does all of that.

Seeing "Google hasn't verified this app"? It hasn't been through Google's review yet. Click Advanced, then Go to college.anshul-anand.com. It asks only for read-only access to your Classroom courses, assignments, announcements and materials.
