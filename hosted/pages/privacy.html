Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

What Classroom Digest reads

With your permission, it reads your Google Classroom courses, the coursework and your own submission status, announcements and course materials, all read-only. It never posts, edits or submits anything, and it does not request access to Drive, Gmail or your calendar.

What it keeps

Nothing is stored on the server. When you sign in, Google's long-lived sign-in token is encrypted and placed in a cookie in your browser. Each time you open the page, the server uses it to read Classroom, sorts the results, sends them to your browser and keeps no copy. Your choice of which courses to hide is kept in a second cookie.

There is no database, no analytics and no advertising. Your data is not sold, shared or used to train AI models. The hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard request logs, which do not include your coursework.

The Claude connector

If you add Classroom Digest as a connector in a Claude app, the same read-only access is used to answer your questions there. The tokens Claude holds are encrypted packets that only this server can open; nothing about you is stored here. Your questions and Claude's answers are handled by Anthropic under its own terms. Remove the connector in Claude to stop it.

Removing access

Press Sign out on the dashboard. That deletes the cookies and revokes the token at Google. You can also remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Classroom Digest's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/nanj-dnd/classroom-digest.
